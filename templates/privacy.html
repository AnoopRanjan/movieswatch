Privacy Policy

Compliance with India DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR • Effective November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect), CinePulse collects only your public display name, email address, and avatar image. We do not store Google account passwords, credit card numbers, or private personal data on our servers.

2. Location Data & Permissions

Cinema ticket discovery utilizes the standard browser HTML5 Geolocation API solely to compute geographical distance (in kilometers using the Haversine formula) to nearby multiplexes. Geolocation coordinates are never stored in databases, tracked across sessions, or sold to third parties.

3. Third-Party Embeds & Cookies

Free movie streaming is delivered via official YouTube IFrame embeds from verified studio channels under YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. AdSense advertising slots operate under strict Google ad policies.

4. User Right to Erasure

Under statutory DPDP regulations, users may permanently delete their account, viewing history, and watchlist at any time directly through the Profile Settings page.
